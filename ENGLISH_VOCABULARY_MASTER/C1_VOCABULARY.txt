  Every English word is taught exactly ONCE in the whole A1–C2 system: as a main word, as a word-family
  form, or as the new word inside one expression. Only grammar words (a, the, of, to, be, have, do ...)
  listed in A1 "Section 0" may recur inside expressions. Example sentences may recycle known words.
  A fixed phrase (get up, at least, in fact) is an item of its own: it is built from words that were taught
  earlier and it is taught once, as a phrase, in a fixed-phrase lesson.

MAIN WORD: ALLEVIATE
Azerbaijani: Yüngülləşdirmək, azaltmaq (ağrını, çətinliyi)

A. MAIN WORD
   English spelling ....... alleviate
   Azerbaijani meaning .... yüngülləşdirmək, azaltmaq (ağrını, çətinliyi)
   Part of speech ......... verb
   Key forms .............. alleviate – alleviates – alleviated – alleviated – alleviating
   British English IPA .... /əˈliːvieɪt/
   CEFR level ............. C1 (Advanced)

B. WORD FAMILY
   Noun forms ........ alleviation — yüngülləşdirmə, azaldılma
   Verb forms ........ alleviate (main word)
   Adjective forms ... —
   Adverb forms ...... —

C. RELATED EXPRESSIONS
   1. Alleviate symptoms — Simptomları yüngülləşdirmək
      Usage: symptom = a change in your body that shows you are ill
   2. Alleviate the burden — Yükü yüngülləşdirmək
      Usage: burden = a heavy responsibility or duty
   3. Alleviate anxiety — Təşvişi (narahatlığı) azaltmaq
      Usage: anxiety = the state of feeling nervous or worried
   4. Alleviate hardship — Çətinliyi (məhrumiyyəti) yüngülləşdirmək
      Usage: hardship = a situation that is difficult because you lack money or food
   5. Alleviate distress — İztirabı yüngülləşdirmək
      Usage: distress = great worry, sadness or pain
   6. Alleviate tension — Gərginliyi azaltmaq
      Usage: tension = a situation in which people do not trust each other
   7. Alleviate overcrowding — Həddindən artıq sıxlığı azaltmaq
      Usage: overcrowding = too many people in one place
   8. Alleviate hunger — Aclığı azaltmaq
      Usage: hunger = the state of not having enough food

D. NATURAL EXAMPLE SENTENCES
   1. The drug is used to alleviate the symptoms of flu.
      → Bu dərman qripin simptomlarını yüngülləşdirmək üçün istifadə olunur.
   2. A second runway would alleviate overcrowding at the airport.
      → İkinci uçuş-enmə zolağı hava limanındakı sıxlığı azaldardı.
   3. The new benefits are intended to alleviate hardship among pensioners.
      → Yeni müavinətlər təqaüdçülərin çətinliyini yüngülləşdirmək məqsədi daşıyır.
   4. Talking to a friend can help to alleviate anxiety.
      → Dostla söhbət etmək təşvişi azaltmağa kömək edə bilər.

E. USAGE EXPLANATION
   ALLEVIATE means to make something bad less severe; it does not mean to remove it completely. It
   is formal and typical of medical, social and economic writing. Its objects are always negative:
   pain, symptoms, poverty, suffering, pressure, congestion. Near synonyms: EASE and RELIEVE (more
   general), MITIGATE (reduce the harmful effects of something), REDUCE (neutral). "Alleviate
   poverty" is one of the most frequent collocations in IELTS essays.

MAIN WORD: ADVOCATE
Azerbaijani: Müdafiə etmək, tərəfdarı olmaq; tərəfdar, müdafiəçi; vəkil

A. MAIN WORD
   English spelling ....... advocate
   Azerbaijani meaning .... müdafiə etmək, tərəfdarı olmaq; tərəfdar, müdafiəçi; vəkil
   Part of speech ......... verb, noun
   Key forms .............. advocate – advocates – advocated – advocated – advocating
   British English IPA .... /ˈædvəkeɪt/ (verb)/, /ˈædvəkət/ (noun)/
   CEFR level ............. C1 (Advanced)

B. WORD FAMILY
   Noun forms ........ advocate (main word); advocacy — müdafiə, təbliğ, həmçinin vəkillik
   Verb forms ........ advocate (main word)
   Adjective forms ... —
   Adverb forms ...... —

C. RELATED EXPRESSIONS
   1. Advocate for — Nəyinsə lehinə çıxış etmək, nəyisə müdafiə etmək
      Usage: verb + FOR; in British English the verb is often used with a direct object instead
   2. A staunch advocate — Sadiq (dönməz) tərəfdar
      Usage: staunch = strong and loyal in your support
   3. A passionate advocate — Ehtiraslı müdafiəçi
      Usage: passionate = having very strong feelings
   4. An outspoken advocate — Fikrini açıq deyən tərəfdar
      Usage: outspoken = saying openly what you think
   5. Advocate a ban — Qadağa tətbiq olunmasını müdafiə etmək
      Usage: ban = an official rule forbidding something
   6. Advocate the abolition of — Nəyinsə ləğv edilməsinin tərəfdarı olmaq
      Usage: abolition = the official ending of a law or system
   7. Devil's advocate — Bilərəkdən əks mövqeyi müdafiə edən adam
      Usage: devil = the spirit of evil; idiom: play devil's advocate
   8. Advocacy on behalf of — Kiminsə adından (maraqları naminə) müdafiə
      Usage: on behalf of = as the representative of

D. NATURAL EXAMPLE SENTENCES
   1. Many doctors advocate a ban on tobacco advertising.
      → Bir çox həkim tütün reklamına qadağa qoyulmasını müdafiə edir.
   2. She is a passionate advocate of free education.
      → O, pulsuz təhsilin ehtiraslı müdafiəçisidir.
   3. The group advocates for the rights of disabled people.
      → Qrup əlilliyi olan şəxslərin hüquqlarını müdafiə edir.
   4. I don't really believe that; I'm just playing devil's advocate.
      → Əslində belə düşünmürəm; sadəcə bilərəkdən əks mövqedən çıxış edirəm.

E. USAGE EXPLANATION
   The verb and the noun are spelt the same but pronounced differently: to ADVOCATE /ˈædvəkeɪt/, an
   ADVOCATE /ˈædvəkət/. The verb means to support an idea publicly and takes a direct object or
   -ing: "advocate reform", "advocate reducing taxes" (not "advocate to reduce"). The noun takes OF
   or FOR: "an advocate of change". In Scotland an advocate is also a lawyer who speaks in court.
   ADVOCACY is the work of publicly supporting a cause.

MAIN WORD: ALLOCATE
Azerbaijani: Ayırmaq, bölüşdürmək, təxsis etmək

   Azerbaijani meaning .... ayırmaq, bölüşdürmək, təxsis etmək
   Part of speech ......... verb
   Key forms .............. allocate – allocates – allocated – allocated – allocating
   British English IPA .... /ˈæləkeɪt/
   CEFR level ............. C1 (Advanced)

B. WORD FAMILY
   Noun forms ........ allocation — ayırma, bölgü, həmçinin ayrılmış pay
   Verb forms ........ allocate (main word)
   Adjective forms ... —
   Adverb forms ...... —

C. RELATED EXPRESSIONS
   1. Allocate subsidies — Subsidiyalar ayırmaq
      Usage: subsidy = money paid by a government to reduce the cost of something
   2. Allocate a portion — Bir hissəsini ayırmaq
      Usage: portion = one part of a larger amount
   3. Allocate land — Torpaq ayırmaq
      Usage: land = an area of ground
   4. Allocate a quota — Kvota ayırmaq
      Usage: quota = a fixed amount that is officially allowed
   5. Allocate duties — Vəzifələri bölüşdürmək
      Usage: duty = a task that is part of your job
   6. Allocate efficiently — Səmərəli bölüşdürmək
      Usage: efficiently = without wasting time, money or energy
   7. Randomly allocated — Təsadüfi qaydada bölüşdürülmüş
      Usage: randomly = without any plan or pattern; common in research
   8. Allocate aid — Yardım ayırmaq
      Usage: aid = money, food or help sent to people in need

D. NATURAL EXAMPLE SENTENCES
   1. The government has allocated two million pounds for the project.
      → Hökumət layihə üçün iki milyon funt ayırıb.
   2. A large portion of the budget is allocated to education.
      → Büdcənin böyük bir hissəsi təhsilə ayrılır.
   3. Patients were randomly allocated to one of two groups.
      → Xəstələr təsadüfi qaydada iki qrupdan birinə bölüşdürüldü.
   4. Markets do not always allocate resources efficiently.
      → Bazarlar ehtiyatları həmişə səmərəli bölüşdürmür.

E. USAGE EXPLANATION
   ALLOCATE means to decide officially that something will be used for a particular purpose or given
   to a particular person. Patterns: allocate something TO somebody or something; allocate something
   FOR a purpose; allocate somebody something ("We were allocated seats at the front"). "Allocate
   resources" is the key collocation in economics. Compare ASSIGN (give a task or role to a person)
   and DISTRIBUTE (share things out among many people).

   Adverb forms ...... ambiguously — ikimənalı şəkildə

C. RELATED EXPRESSIONS
   1. Deliberately ambiguous — Qəsdən ikimənalı (qeyri-müəyyən)
      Usage: deliberately = on purpose
   2. Somewhat ambiguous — Bir qədər qeyri-müəyyən
      Usage: somewhat = to some degree; a formal hedge
   3. An ambiguous phrase — İkimənalı ifadə
      Usage: phrase = a group of words used together
   4. An ambiguous ending — İkimənalı (açıq) sonluq
      Usage: ending = the last part of a story or film
   5. An ambiguous gesture — İkimənalı jest
      Usage: gesture = a movement of the hand or head that shows meaning
   6. An ambiguous response — Qeyri-müəyyən cavab
      Usage: response = an answer or reaction
   7. Remove ambiguity — İkimənalılığı aradan qaldırmaq
      Usage: remove = take away
   8. Clear and unambiguous — Aydın və birmənalı
      Usage: clear = easy to understand

D. NATURAL EXAMPLE SENTENCES
   1. The wording of the contract is deliberately ambiguous.
      → Müqavilənin mətni qəsdən ikimənalı tərtib olunub.
   2. The film has an ambiguous ending.
      → Filmin sonluğu ikimənalıdır.
   3. His response to the question was somewhat ambiguous.
      → Onun suala cavabı bir qədər qeyri-müəyyən idi.
   4. Instructions must be clear and unambiguous.
      → Təlimatlar aydın və birmənalı olmalıdır.

E. USAGE EXPLANATION
   AMBIGUOUS describes language or a situation that can be understood in more than one way. Do not
   confuse it with AMBIVALENT, which describes a person who has mixed feelings ("She is ambivalent
   about the move"). VAGUE means not clear or detailed; AMBIGUOUS means having two or more possible
   meanings. The noun AMBIGUITY is countable for particular cases ("several ambiguities in the
   text") and uncountable for the quality.

   3. A coherent framework — Bitkin çərçivə (konsepsiya)

   4. A coherent vision — Aydın və ardıcıl baxış (gələcək mənzərəsi)

      Usage: The name of one of the four IELTS Writing marking criteria
   8. Incoherent with rage — Qəzəbdən sözünü itirmiş
      Usage: rage = extreme anger

   1. The government lacks a coherent economic policy.
      → Hökumətin ardıcıl iqtisadi siyasəti yoxdur.
   2. Each paragraph should have a coherent structure.
      → Hər abzasın məntiqli quruluşu olmalıdır.

      → O qədər yorğun idi ki, çətinliklə rabitəli danışırdı.

E. USAGE EXPLANATION
   COHERENT describes ideas, plans or writing whose parts fit together logically. In IELTS Writing,

   pronouns and paragraphing to connect sentences. A person who is INCOHERENT cannot speak clearly,
   because of emotion, illness or drink. Compare CONSISTENT (not contradicting itself; always
   behaving in the same way).

MAIN WORD: COMPELLING

   English spelling ....... compelling
   Azerbaijani meaning .... inandırıcı, tutarlı; cəlbedici, diqqəti çəkən

   Verb forms ........ compel — məcbur etmək
   Adjective forms ... compelling (main word); compulsory — məcburi, icbari
   Adverb forms ...... —

C. RELATED EXPRESSIONS
   1. A compelling case — Tutarlı əsaslandırma (dəlillər toplusu)
      Usage: case = a set of facts and arguments supporting one side
   2. A compelling portrait — Təsirli portret (təsvir)
      Usage: portrait = a painting or detailed description of a person
   3. Utterly compelling — Son dərəcə cəlbedici
      Usage: utterly = completely; used for emphasis
   4. A compelling justification — Tutarlı əsas (haqq qazandıran səbəb)
      Usage: justification = a good reason for doing something
   5. Be compelled to — Nəyisə etməyə məcbur olmaq
      Usage: Passive of "compel" + to-infinitive
   6. Under no compulsion to — Nəyisə etməyə heç bir məcburiyyəti olmamaq
      Usage: Formal fixed phrase
   7. Compel sb to testify — Kimisə ifadə verməyə məcbur etmək
      Usage: testify = make a formal statement in a court of law
   8. Compulsory attendance — Məcburi davamiyyət
      Usage: attendance = being present at school, work or an event

D. NATURAL EXAMPLE SENTENCES
   1. The report makes a compelling case for investing in early education.
      → Hesabat erkən təhsilə sərmayə qoyulmasının vacibliyini tutarlı şəkildə əsaslandırır.
   2. The film is utterly compelling from start to finish.
      → Film əvvəldən axıra qədər son dərəcə cəlbedicidir.
   3. I felt compelled to tell her the truth.
      → Mən ona həqiqəti deməyə məcbur olduğumu hiss etdim.
   4. You are under no compulsion to answer these questions.
      → Bu suallara cavab verməyə heç bir məcburiyyətiniz yoxdur.

E. USAGE EXPLANATION
   COMPELLING has two senses: so interesting that you cannot stop paying attention (a compelling
   story), and so strong that you must accept it (a compelling argument, compelling evidence). The
   verb COMPEL is formal and usually passive: "be / feel compelled TO do something". COMPULSORY is
   the everyday adjective for something required by a rule or law (compulsory education); its
   opposite is VOLUNTARY or OPTIONAL.
