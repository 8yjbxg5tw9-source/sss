   than it is). Near synonyms: AGGRAVATE, WORSEN, COMPOUND, INFLAME. Opposites: ALLEVIATE,
   AMELIORATE, EASE.

      → Yeni mənzil proqramı şəhər mərkəzlərindəki məhrumiyyəti azaltmaq məqsədi daşıyır.

   mainly in academic, legal and medical texts; in ordinary writing use IMPROVE or EASE. Its objects

   suffering less severe) and MITIGATE (reduce the seriousness of harm). The direct opposite is
   EXACERBATE.

      → Yoxsulların işləmək istəməməsi zərərli bir mifdir.

      → Müharibə özündən sonra nifrətdən ibarət ağır miras qoydu.

   Compare INSIDIOUS (spreading secretly), DETRIMENTAL (harmful, neutral in tone) and MALIGN (evil
   in effect). "Pernicious anaemia" is a medical term.

   with weapons ("nuclear proliferation", "the Non-Proliferation Treaty"), but it is used for
   anything that multiplies quickly: the proliferation of mobile apps. Synonyms: MULTIPLY, MUSHROOM
   (informal), SPREAD.

      Usage: passage = a short section of a book or speech

   SALIENT means most noticeable or important. It is formal and usually comes before the noun: the
   salient points, the salient features, the salient facts. "The salient features" is exactly what
