THE NO-REPETITION RULE  /  TƏKRARSIZLIQ QAYDASI
  Every English word is taught exactly ONCE in the whole A1–C2 system: as a main word, as a word-family
  form, or as the new word inside one expression. Only grammar words (a, the, of, to, be, have, do ...)
  listed in A1 "Section 0" may recur inside expressions. Example sentences may recycle known words.
  A fixed phrase (get up, at least, in fact) is an item of its own: it is built from words that were taught
  earlier and it is taught once, as a phrase, in a fixed-phrase lesson.
  Hər bir ingilis sözü bütün sistemdə (A1–C2) yalnız BİR dəfə öyrədilir. Yalnız A1 faylının
  "Section 0" bölməsindəki qrammatik sözlər ifadələrin içində təkrarlana bilər.

MAIN WORD: ACHIEVE
Azerbaijani: Nail olmaq, əldə etmək, (məqsədə) çatmaq

A. MAIN WORD
   English spelling ....... achieve
   Azerbaijani meaning .... nail olmaq, əldə etmək, (məqsədə) çatmaq
   Part of speech ......... verb
   Key forms .............. achieve – achieves – achieved – achieved – achieving
   British English IPA .... /əˈtʃiːv/
   CEFR level ............. B1 (Intermediate)
   Level note ............. The headword on its own is listed at A2 in the open reference wordlist;
                           this lesson is B1 because the expressions built on it are B1 material.

B. WORD FAMILY
   Noun forms ........ achievement — nailiyyət; achiever — nailiyyət qazanan şəxs
   Verb forms ........ achieve (main word)
   Adjective forms ... achievable — əldə edilə bilən, real
   Adverb forms ...... —

C. RELATED EXPRESSIONS
   1. Achieve a goal — Məqsədə çatmaq
      Usage: The most common collocation; goal = something you plan and work for
   2. Achieve results — Nəticə əldə etmək
      Usage: Often with an adjective: achieve good / impressive results
   3. Achieve an aim — Məqsədə nail olmaq
      Usage: aim = purpose; slightly more formal than "goal"
   4. Achieve your ambition — Arzusuna (iddiasına) çatmaq
      Usage: ambition = a strong wish to succeed
   5. Achieve independence — Müstəqillik əldə etmək
      Usage: Used of countries and of young people
   6. Achieve a victory — Qələbə qazanmaq
      Usage: Formal; in sport, war and politics
   7. Achieve excellent grades — Əla qiymətlər almaq
      Usage: grade = a mark for school or university work
   8. Achieve fame — Şöhrət qazanmaq
      Usage: fame = the state of being known by many people

D. NATURAL EXAMPLE SENTENCES
   1. She worked hard and achieved her goal.
      → O, səylə çalışdı və məqsədinə çatdı.
   2. Azerbaijan achieved independence in 1991.
      → Azərbaycan 1991-ci ildə müstəqillik əldə etdi.
   3. You will never achieve good results without practice.
      → Məşq etmədən heç vaxt yaxşı nəticə əldə edə bilməzsən.
   4. He achieved fame as a writer at the age of thirty.
      → O, otuz yaşında yazıçı kimi şöhrət qazandı.

E. USAGE EXPLANATION
   ACHIEVE means to succeed in doing something after effort, so it combines with nouns for things
   you aim at: a goal, an aim, results, an ambition. It is not used for receiving things by chance.
   Compare REACH (reach a decision, reach a level) and GAIN (gain experience, gain confidence).
   ACHIEVE is neutral to formal and is very common in essays, CVs and reports. The noun is
   ACHIEVEMENT: "a great achievement".

   Verb forms ........ succeed (main word)
   Adjective forms ... successful — uğurlu; unsuccessful — uğursuz

C. RELATED EXPRESSIONS
   1. Succeed in doing sth — Nəyisə bacarmaq, bir işdə uğur qazanmaq
      Usage: Pattern: succeed IN + -ing (never "succeed to do")
   2. Without success — Uğursuz, nəticəsiz
      Usage: Adverbial phrase: I tried, but without success
   3. Highly successful — Çox uğurlu, olduqca uğurlu
      Usage: "highly" is the usual intensifier here
   4. A commercial success — Kommersiya uğuru
      Usage: Of films, books and products that make money
   5. Wish sb success — Kiməsə uğur arzulamaq
      Usage: Formal good wishes: I wish you every success
   6. A successful outcome — Uğurlu nəticə
      Usage: outcome = the final result of a process
   7. Academic success — Təhsildə uğur, akademik uğur
      Usage: academic = connected with study
   8. Celebrate success — Uğuru qeyd etmək
      Usage: celebrate = mark a happy event

D. NATURAL EXAMPLE SENTENCES
   1. He succeeded in passing his driving test.
      → O, sürücülük imtahanından keçə bildi.
   2. I tried to call her several times, but without success.
      → Ona bir neçə dəfə zəng etməyə çalışdım, amma alınmadı.
   3. The film was a huge commercial success.
      → Film böyük kommersiya uğuru qazandı.
   4. We wish you every success in your new job.
      → Yeni işinizdə sizə uğurlar arzulayırıq.

E. USAGE EXPLANATION
   SUCCEED is followed by IN + -ing, not by an infinitive: "She succeeded in finding a job." The
   word family is frequent and easy to confuse: SUCCESS is the noun (/səkˈses/), SUCCESSFUL the
   adjective, SUCCESSFULLY the adverb. A person, a plan or a product can be "a success". The
   opposite verb is FAIL and the opposite noun is FAILURE.

E. USAGE EXPLANATION
   As a noun, MIND is the part of you that thinks and remembers; it is close to "ağıl" and "fikir",
   not to BRAIN (the physical organ). Key idioms: change your mind (fikrini dəyişmək), make up your

   mind + -ing?" is a polite request, and the polite positive answer is "No, not at all". "Mind the
   gap!" is a warning.

C. RELATED EXPRESSIONS
   1. Deal with — Bir işlə məşğul olmaq, həll etmək, öhdəsindən gəlmək
      Usage: The key phrasal verb: deal with a problem / a customer
   2. It's a deal — Razılaşdıq, oldu
      Usage: Spoken; said when you accept an offer
   3. Do a deal — Sövdələşmək, razılığa gəlmək
      Usage: British English; also "make a deal"
   4. A fair deal — Ədalətli sövdələşmə; ədalətli rəftar
      Usage: fair = treating everyone equally

      Usage: strike = here: reach (journalistic)

      Usage: Informal; something already decided

      Usage: art = paintings, drawings and sculpture
   8. Deal with an emergency — Fövqəladə vəziyyətin öhdəsindən gəlmək
      Usage: emergency = a sudden dangerous situation

D. NATURAL EXAMPLE SENTENCES
   1. How do you deal with difficult customers?
      → Çətin müştərilərlə necə davranırsan?
   2. The two companies have struck a deal.
      → İki şirkət razılıq əldə edib.
   3. I'll cook if you wash the dishes. – It's a deal!
      → Sən qabları yusan, mən yemək bişirərəm. – Razılaşdıq!

      → Heyət fövqəladə vəziyyətlərin öhdəsindən gəlmək üçün təlim keçib.

E. USAGE EXPLANATION
   DEAL is irregular: deal – dealt – dealt, pronounced /delt/. DEAL WITH has three meanings: take
   action to solve (deal with a problem), do business with (deal with a supplier) and be about (The
   book deals with war). The noun DEAL is a business agreement; "a great deal of" means "a lot of"
   and is used with uncountable nouns in formal English: a great deal of money. "It's no big deal"
   means it is not important.
