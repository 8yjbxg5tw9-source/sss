## Society, innovation and the environment | Cəmiyyət, innovasiya və ətraf mühit
@ CONTROVERSIAL | adjective | ˌkɒntrəˈvɜːʃəl | mübahisəli, ziddiyyətli fikirlər doğuran
n: controversy = mübahisə, ixtilaf, qalmaqal
- a controversial figure = birmənalı qarşılanmayan şəxsiyyət :: figure = a well-known person
- a controversial proposal = mübahisəli təklif :: proposal = a formal plan or suggestion
- a controversial remark = mübahisə doğuran söz (replika) :: remark = something that you say; a comment
- spark controversy = mübahisə doğurmaq :: spark = cause something to start suddenly
- the controversy surrounding = nəyinsə ətrafında yaranan mübahisə :: surround = be closely connected with
- controversy over = nəyləsə bağlı mübahisə :: controversy OVER / ABOUT something
- remain controversial = mübahisəli olaraq qalmaq :: remain = continue to be
- a controversial scheme = mübahisəli layihə (plan) :: scheme = an official plan; British English
> The minister's controversial remarks angered many teachers. = Nazirin mübahisə doğuran sözləri bir çox müəllimi qəzəbləndirdi.
> The new law has sparked controversy across the country. = Yeni qanun bütün ölkədə mübahisə doğurub.
> He remains a controversial figure in British politics. = O, Britaniya siyasətində birmənalı qarşılanmayan şəxsiyyət olaraq qalır.
> The controversy surrounding the film helped to sell tickets. = Filmin ətrafında yaranan mübahisə biletlərin satılmasına kömək etdi.
! CONTROVERSIAL describes something that causes strong public disagreement; it does not mean "wrong". British speakers stress the noun in two ways: CONtroversy and conTROVersy. Verbs with the noun: cause, spark, stir up, arouse controversy; a controversy arises. In IELTS essays, "a controversial issue" and "this remains controversial" are useful for introducing a debate.

@ CAMPAIGN | noun, verb | kæmˈpeɪn | kampaniya; kampaniya aparmaq, mübarizə aparmaq
forms: campaign – campaigns – campaigned – campaigned – campaigning
n: campaigner = kampaniya fəalı, mübariz
- launch a campaign = kampaniyaya başlamaq :: launch = start an activity, especially an organised one
- an election campaign = seçki kampaniyası :: election = choosing a person for a position by voting
- an advertising campaign = reklam kampaniyası :: advertising = telling the public about products
- campaign against racism = irqçiliyə qarşı mübarizə aparmaq :: racism = unfair treatment of people of other races
- a fundraising campaign = vəsait toplama kampaniyası :: fundraising = collecting money for a charity or cause
- a campaign slogan = kampaniya şüarı :: slogan = a short phrase that is easy to remember
- a nationwide campaign = ümumölkə kampaniyası :: nationwide = happening in all parts of a country
- a tireless campaigner = yorulmaz mübariz :: tireless = working very hard without stopping
> The government has launched a campaign to reduce smoking. = Hökumət siqaretçəkmənin azaldılması üçün kampaniyaya başlayıb.
> The party spent millions on its election campaign. = Partiya seçki kampaniyasına milyonlar xərclədi.
> She has campaigned against racism all her life. = O, bütün ömrü boyu irqçiliyə qarşı mübarizə aparıb.
> He was a tireless campaigner for children's rights. = O, uşaq hüquqları uğrunda yorulmaz mübariz idi.
! The "g" in CAMPAIGN is silent: /kæmˈpeɪn/. A campaign is a planned series of activities with a particular aim: political, commercial or social. As a verb it takes FOR or AGAINST: campaign FOR equal pay, campaign AGAINST a new road. Typical verbs with the noun: launch, run, lead, mount, support a campaign.

@ SUSTAIN | verb | səˈsteɪn | davam etdirmək, qoruyub saxlamaq; (zərər, xəsarət) almaq
forms: sustain – sustains – sustained – sustained – sustaining
n: sustainability = davamlılıq
adj: sustainable = davamlı, ətraf mühitə ziyan vurmayan; unsustainable = davamlı olmayan, belə davam edə bilməyən; sustained = uzunmüddətli, fasiləsiz
- sustainable agriculture = davamlı kənd təsərrüfatı :: agriculture = farming
- sustainable sources = davamlı (tükənməyən) mənbələr :: source = a place or thing that you get something from
- sustainable materials = ekoloji cəhətdən davamlı materiallar :: material = a substance that things are made from
- sustain momentum = sürəti (tempi) qoruyub saxlamaq :: momentum = the force that keeps a process developing
- sustain the pace = tempi saxlamaq :: pace = the speed at which something happens
- a sustained attack = fasiləsiz hücum :: attack = an act of violence or strong criticism
- ultimately unsustainable = son nəticədə davam etdirilməsi mümkün olmayan :: ultimately = in the end
- a commitment to sustainability = davamlılığa sadiqlik :: commitment = a promise to support something
> We need to get more of our energy from sustainable sources. = Enerjimizin daha çox hissəsini davamlı mənbələrdən əldə etməliyik.
> The team could not sustain the pace in the second half. = Komanda ikinci hissədə tempi saxlaya bilmədi.
> Such high levels of debt are ultimately unsustainable. = Borcun belə yüksək səviyyəsi son nəticədə davam edə bilməz.
> Two passengers sustained serious injuries in the crash. = Qəzada iki sərnişin ağır xəsarət aldı.
! SUSTAIN has three uses: 1) make something continue (sustain growth, sustain interest); 2) formally, suffer something bad (sustain an injury, sustain damage); 3) keep alive (enough food to sustain life). SUSTAINABLE is a key word for environmental topics: sustainable development means economic growth that does not destroy natural resources. SUSTAINED is the adjective for something that continues for a long time: a sustained effort.

@ INNOVATE | verb | ˈɪnəveɪt | yenilik etmək, yeniliklər tətbiq etmək
forms: innovate – innovates – innovated – innovated – innovating
n: innovation = yenilik, innovasiya; innovator = yenilikçi, novator
adj: innovative = yenilikçi, innovativ
- an innovative technique = yenilikçi üsul (texnika) :: technique = a particular way of doing something that needs skill
- an innovative concept = yenilikçi konsepsiya :: concept = an idea or principle
- an innovative feature = yenilikçi xüsusiyyət (funksiya) :: feature = an important part or quality of something
- an innovative strategy = innovativ strategiya :: strategy = a plan intended to achieve a purpose
- encourage innovation = yenilikləri təşviq etmək :: encourage = give support and hope to
- a hub of innovation = innovasiya mərkəzi :: hub = the central point of an activity
- constantly innovate = daim yenilik etmək :: constantly = all the time
- embrace innovation = yenilikləri həvəslə qəbul etmək :: embrace = accept an idea with enthusiasm
> Companies must constantly innovate to stay ahead of their rivals. = Şirkətlər rəqiblərini qabaqlamaq üçün daim yenilik etməlidirlər.
> The phone has several innovative features. = Telefonun bir neçə yenilikçi funksiyası var.
> The city has become a hub of innovation. = Şəhər innovasiya mərkəzinə çevrilib.
> Lower taxes are intended to encourage innovation. = Aşağı vergilər yenilikləri təşviq etmək məqsədi daşıyır.
! INNOVATE means to introduce new ideas, methods or products; it is usually intransitive. An INVENTION is a completely new thing; an INNOVATION may be a new way of using existing things. The adjective INNOVATIVE is pronounced /ˈɪnəvətɪv/ or /ˈɪnəveɪtɪv/. INNOVATION is uncountable for the process ("encourage innovation") and countable for a single new idea ("the latest innovations").

@ CLIMATE | noun | ˈklaɪmət | iqlim; ab-hava, mühit
- a mild climate = mülayim iqlim :: mild = not very cold or very hot
- a tropical climate = tropik iqlim :: tropical = of the hottest parts of the world
- a harsh climate = sərt iqlim :: harsh = severe and unpleasant
- a climate of fear = qorxu mühiti (ab-havası) :: fear = the feeling of being afraid; figurative use of "climate"
- a climate summit = iqlim sammiti :: summit = an official meeting between leaders
- a humid climate = rütubətli iqlim :: humid = warm and damp
~ greenhouse gases = istixana qazları :: greenhouse = a glass building for plants; gas = a substance like air
~ a carbon footprint = karbon izi :: carbon = the element in coal and CO2; footprint = the mark left by a foot
> Lankaran has a mild, humid climate. = Lənkəranın mülayim, rütubətli iqlimi var.
> Climate change is the greatest threat facing our planet. = İqlim dəyişikliyi planetimizin üzləşdiyi ən böyük təhlükədir.
> World leaders will meet at the climate summit in November. = Dünya liderləri noyabrda iqlim sammitində görüşəcəklər.
> You can reduce your carbon footprint by flying less. = Daha az uçmaqla karbon izinizi azalda bilərsiniz.
! WEATHER is what happens today (rain, sun); CLIMATE is the typical weather of a place over many years. "Climate change" is the fixed term for long-term changes in the Earth's temperature; "global warming" is one part of it. CLIMATE is also used figuratively for the general mood or situation: the political climate, the current economic climate, a climate of fear. The pronunciation is /ˈklaɪmət/ – the last syllable is weak.

@ MOTIVATE | verb | ˈməʊtɪveɪt | həvəsləndirmək, motivasiya etmək; sövq etmək
forms: motivate – motivates – motivated – motivated – motivating
n: motivation = motivasiya, həvəs; motive = motiv, səbəb, niyyət
adj: motivated = həvəsli, motivasiyalı; self-motivated = öz-özünü həvəsləndirən, təşəbbüskar
- motivate pupils = şagirdləri həvəsləndirmək :: pupil = a child at school (British English)
- motivate the workforce = işçi heyətini həvəsləndirmək :: workforce = all the people who work for a company
- the motivation behind = nəyinsə arxasında duran səbəb (motiv) :: behind = here: being the reason for
- a hidden motive = gizli niyyət :: hidden = kept secret; from "hide"
- an ulterior motive = gizli (məkrli) niyyət :: ulterior = kept secret because it is selfish; used almost only with "motive"
- motivate sb to do sth = kimisə nəyisə etməyə həvəsləndirmək :: Pattern: motivate + object + to-infinitive
- motivated by greed = tamahkarlıqdan irəli gələn :: greed = a strong wish for more money or food than you need
- inspire and motivate = ruhlandırmaq və həvəsləndirmək :: inspire = fill somebody with the wish to do something
> A good teacher knows how to motivate pupils. = Yaxşı müəllim şagirdləri necə həvəsləndirməyi bilir.
> What was the motivation behind his decision? = Onun qərarının arxasında duran səbəb nə idi?
> I think he had an ulterior motive for helping us. = Məncə, bizə kömək etməkdə onun gizli niyyəti var idi.
> The crime was motivated by greed. = Cinayət tamahkarlıq zəminində törədilmişdi.
! To MOTIVATE somebody is to make them want to do something; a MOTIVE is the reason for an action, especially a crime; MOTIVATION is the desire to do something or the reason for it. "Highly motivated" is the standard description in job applications. The passive "be motivated BY" names the reason: motivated by money, by fear, by a desire to help. Pattern: motivate somebody TO do something.

@ ATTITUDE | noun | ˈætɪtjuːd | münasibət, yanaşma, mövqe
- attitude towards = kiməsə və ya nəyəsə münasibət :: attitude TOWARDS / TO somebody or something
- a casual attitude = laqeyd (qeyri-ciddi) münasibət :: casual = not showing much care or thought
- a hostile attitude = düşməncəsinə münasibət :: hostile = unfriendly and aggressive
- a mature attitude = yetkin yanaşma :: mature = behaving in a sensible, adult way
- a can-do attitude = "bacararam" ruhu, işgüzar əhval-ruhiyyə :: can-do = confident and willing to deal with problems
- a laid-back attitude = rahat (arxayın) münasibət :: laid-back = calm and relaxed; informal
- attitudes vary = münasibətlər müxtəlifdir :: vary = be different from each other
- reflect attitudes = münasibətləri əks etdirmək :: reflect = show the nature of something
> His attitude towards work has changed completely. = Onun işə münasibəti tamamilə dəyişib.
> Attitudes to marriage vary from country to country. = Evliliyə münasibət ölkədən ölkəyə dəyişir.
> She has a very mature attitude for her age. = Yaşına görə onun çox yetkin yanaşması var.
> The film reflects the attitudes of the 1960s. = Film 1960-cı illərin baxışlarını əks etdirir.
! ATTITUDE is the way you think and feel about something, shown in your behaviour. The prepositions are TO and TOWARDS: "attitudes TO immigration". The basic adjectives are POSITIVE and NEGATIVE: "a positive attitude". In informal English, "an attitude problem" or simply "attitude" means unhelpful, rude behaviour. Do not confuse it with APTITUDE (natural ability).

@ ESTABLISH | verb | ɪˈstæblɪʃ | təsis etmək, qurmaq, yaratmaq; müəyyən etmək
forms: establish – establishes – established – established – establishing
n: establishment = təsis edilmə, həmçinin müəssisə, idarə
adj: established = tanınmış, oturuşmuş; well-established = möhkəm oturuşmuş, çoxdan tanınan
- establish ties = əlaqələr qurmaq :: tie = a strong connection between people or countries
- establish a connection = əlaqə yaratmaq :: connection = something that links two things
- establish the identity = şəxsiyyətini müəyyən etmək :: identity = who a person is
- establish a reputation = ad çıxarmaq, nüfuz qazanmaq :: reputation = the opinion people have of you
- establish criteria = meyarlar müəyyən etmək :: criteria = standards for judging; singular: criterion
- the establishment of democracy = demokratiyanın bərqərar olması :: democracy = government by elected representatives
- firmly established = möhkəm oturuşmuş :: firmly = in a strong, definite way
- establish priorities = prioritetləri müəyyən etmək :: priority = something more important than other things
> The company was established in 1995. = Şirkət 1995-ci ildə təsis edilib.
> Police are trying to establish the identity of the victim. = Polis zərərçəkənin şəxsiyyətini müəyyən etməyə çalışır.
> She quickly established a reputation as a reliable lawyer. = O, tezliklə etibarlı hüquqşünas kimi ad çıxardı.
> The two countries established diplomatic ties in 1992. = İki ölkə 1992-ci ildə diplomatik əlaqələr qurdu.
! ESTABLISH has two main meanings: 1) start an organisation, system or relationship that is meant to last (establish a company, establish ties); 2) discover or prove the facts (establish the cause, establish that ...). It is more formal than "set up". "The Establishment" (with a capital letter) means the powerful people who control a country. WELL-ESTABLISHED describes something that has existed successfully for a long time.

@ MAINTAIN | verb | meɪnˈteɪn | saxlamaq, qoruyub saxlamaq; texniki qulluq göstərmək; iddia etmək
forms: maintain – maintains – maintained – maintained – maintaining
n: maintenance = texniki qulluq, saxlanma
- maintain discipline = intizamı qorumaq :: discipline = the practice of making people obey rules
- maintain silence = sükutu qorumaq :: silence = complete quiet
- maintain your dignity = ləyaqətini qorumaq :: dignity = calm behaviour that makes people respect you
- maintain the status quo = mövcud vəziyyəti qoruyub saxlamaq :: status quo = the situation as it is now (Latin)
- maintain a vehicle = nəqliyyat vasitəsinə texniki qulluq göstərmək :: vehicle = a car, bus, lorry, etc.
- maintain that = təkidlə iddia etmək ki :: maintain (that) + clause = keep saying that something is true
- routine maintenance = planlı (müntəzəm) texniki qulluq :: routine = done regularly as part of normal work
- a maintenance crew = texniki xidmət briqadası :: crew = a group of people working together
> It is important to maintain a healthy weight. = Sağlam çəkini qoruyub saxlamaq vacibdir.
> The teacher found it hard to maintain discipline. = Müəllim intizamı qorumaqda çətinlik çəkirdi.
> He has always maintained that he is innocent. = O, həmişə günahsız olduğunu iddia edib.
> The lift is closed for routine maintenance. = Lift planlı texniki qulluq üçün bağlıdır.
! MAINTAIN has three uses: 1) make something continue at the same level (maintain standards, maintain contact); 2) keep a machine, building or road in good condition; 3) formally, insist that something is true (maintain that ...). The noun is MAINTENANCE – notice the spelling change: maintAIN but maintENance, pronounced /ˈmeɪntənəns/. "Low-maintenance" describes something that needs little care.

@ REVEAL | verb | rɪˈviːl | aşkar etmək, üzə çıxarmaq, açıqlamaq
forms: reveal – reveals – revealed – revealed – revealing
n: revelation = ifşa, gözlənilməz açıqlama
adj: revealing = çox şey açıb göstərən
- reveal a flaw = qüsuru üzə çıxarmaq :: flaw = a fault or weakness
- reveal a scandal = qalmaqalı ifşa etmək :: scandal = behaviour that shocks the public
- reveal a plot = sui-qəsdi (gizli planı) üzə çıxarmaq :: plot = a secret plan to do something wrong
- reveal your intentions = niyyətini açıqlamaq :: intention = what you plan to do
- a survey reveals = sorğu göstərir ki :: survey = a set of questions asked to many people
- a shocking revelation = sarsıdıcı açıqlama (ifşa) :: shocking = very surprising and upsetting
- a revealing insight = çox şey deyən müşahidə :: insight = a clear understanding of something
- refuse to reveal = açıqlamaqdan imtina etmək :: refuse = say that you will not do something
> The report revealed serious flaws in the system. = Hesabat sistemdəki ciddi qüsurları üzə çıxardı.
> A recent survey reveals that most people are worried about prices. = Son sorğu göstərir ki, insanların çoxu qiymətlərdən narahatdır.
> The journalist refused to reveal her sources. = Jurnalist mənbələrini açıqlamaqdan imtina etdi.
> The book contains shocking revelations about the royal family. = Kitabda kral ailəsi haqqında sarsıdıcı açıqlamalar var.
! REVEAL means to make known something that was secret or hidden. Patterns: reveal something TO somebody; reveal THAT + clause; reveal how / why / what. It is common in reporting research: "The study revealed that ...". A REVELATION is a surprising fact that is made known; "It was a revelation to me" means it changed how I understood something. REVEALING also describes clothes that show a lot of the body.
