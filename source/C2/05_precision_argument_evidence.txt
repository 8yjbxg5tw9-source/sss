## Precision in argument and evidence | Arqument və sübutda dəqiqlik
@ ANOMALY | noun | əˈnɒməli | anomaliya, qaydadan kənarlıq, uyğunsuzluq
forms: anomaly – anomalies
adj: anomalous = anomal, qaydadan kənar
- a historical anomaly = tarixi anomaliya (tarixdən qalma uyğunsuzluq) :: historical = connected with the past
- an apparent anomaly = zahiri uyğunsuzluq :: apparent = seeming to be real, but perhaps not
- a strange anomaly = qəribə anomaliya :: strange = unusual or surprising
- an anomaly arises = uyğunsuzluq yaranır :: arise = begin to exist
- a congenital anomaly = anadangəlmə anomaliya :: congenital = existing from birth
- spot an anomaly = uyğunsuzluğu sezmək :: spot = notice
- rectify an anomaly = uyğunsuzluğu aradan qaldırmaq :: rectify = put right something that is wrong
- iron out anomalies = uyğunsuzluqları yoluna qoymaq (hamarlamaq) :: iron out = remove small problems; an iron is a tool for making clothes smooth
> The results contained an anomaly that nobody could explain. = Nəticələrdə heç kimin izah edə bilmədiyi bir anomaliya var idi.
> It is a historical anomaly that the two villages have different tax rules. = İki kəndin fərqli vergi qaydalarına malik olması tarixi anomaliyadır.
> The software is designed to spot anomalies in bank accounts. = Proqram bank hesablarındakı uyğunsuzluqları sezmək üçün nəzərdə tutulub.
> The new law will rectify this anomaly. = Yeni qanun bu uyğunsuzluğu aradan qaldıracaq.
! An ANOMALY is something that is different from what is normal or expected: an anomaly IN the data, an anomaly IN the system. It is common in science, statistics and law. ANOMALOUS is the adjective: "an anomalous result". Compare IRREGULARITY (something that does not follow the rules), ABERRATION (a temporary departure from what is normal) and OUTLIER (a value far from the others in a set of data).

@ ARBITRARY | adjective | ˈɑːbɪtrəri | əsassız, özbaşına, təsadüfi seçilmiş
n: arbitrariness = özbaşınalıq, əsassızlıq
adv: arbitrarily = özbaşına, əsassız olaraq
- arbitrary detention = özbaşına (qanunsuz) həbs :: detention = the state of being kept in prison
- an arbitrary threshold = şərti (əsassız) hədd :: threshold = the level at which something starts to happen
- an arbitrary cut-off = şərti son hədd :: cut-off = a point or limit at which something stops
- arbitrary dismissal = əsassız işdən çıxarılma :: dismissal = the act of removing somebody from a job
- arbitrary punishment = özbaşına cəza :: punishment = a penalty for doing something wrong
- an arbitrary whim = özbaşına şıltaqlıq :: whim = a sudden wish to do something unusual
- arbitrarily assigned = təsadüfi (əsassız) şəkildə təyin edilmiş :: assign = give somebody a task or put them in a group
- arbitrary categories = şərti kateqoriyalar :: category = a group of things with similar features
> The choice of forty as the pass mark seems arbitrary. = Keçid balı kimi qırxın seçilməsi əsassız görünür.
> Human rights groups condemned the arbitrary detention of journalists. = Hüquq müdafiə təşkilatları jurnalistlərin özbaşına həbsini pislədi.
> Students were arbitrarily assigned to one of three groups. = Tələbələr təsadüfi qaydada üç qrupdan birinə təyin edildi.
> The law protects employees against arbitrary dismissal. = Qanun işçiləri əsassız işdən çıxarılmadan qoruyur.
! ARBITRARY describes a decision or rule that is not based on a reason, system or plan and therefore often seems unfair. In law and politics it means using power without restriction: arbitrary arrest, arbitrary rule. Do not confuse it with ARBITRATION (settling a dispute through an independent person). Compare RANDOM (without any pattern), CAPRICIOUS (changing suddenly) and DISCRETIONARY (decided by somebody's judgement).

@ BURGEON | verb | ˈbɜːdʒən | sürətlə inkişaf etmək, çiçəklənmək
forms: burgeon – burgeons – burgeoned – burgeoned – burgeoning
adj: burgeoning = sürətlə böyüyən, çiçəklənən
- a burgeoning romance = yenicə alovlanan sevgi macərası :: romance = a love affair
- a burgeoning sector = sürətlə böyüyən sektor :: sector = a part of a country's economic activity
- a burgeoning metropolis = sürətlə böyüyən nəhəng şəhər :: metropolis = a very large and important city
- a burgeoning deficit = sürətlə artan kəsir :: deficit = the amount by which spending is greater than income
- burgeoning exports = sürətlə artan ixrac :: export = a product sold to another country
- a burgeoning empire = sürətlə genişlənən imperiya :: empire = a group of countries or companies controlled by one ruler or owner
- a burgeoning workload = getdikcə artan iş yükü :: workload = the amount of work that has to be done
- burgeon into = böyüyüb nəyəsə çevrilmək :: burgeon INTO something larger
> Baku is a burgeoning metropolis on the Caspian Sea. = Bakı Xəzər dənizi sahilində sürətlə böyüyən nəhəng şəhərdir.
> The country has a burgeoning technology sector. = Ölkənin sürətlə böyüyən texnologiya sektoru var.
> The small business burgeoned into an international company. = Kiçik müəssisə böyüyüb beynəlxalq şirkətə çevrildi.
> The government is struggling with a burgeoning deficit. = Hökumət sürətlə artan kəsirlə mübarizə aparır.
! BURGEON is a literary and journalistic verb meaning to begin to grow or develop rapidly. It is used far more often as the adjective BURGEONING before a noun: a burgeoning population, a burgeoning market, burgeoning demand. Originally it described plants putting out new buds. Synonyms: FLOURISH, THRIVE, BOOM, MUSHROOM. It is intransitive: things burgeon; you cannot "burgeon something".

@ COGENT | adjective | ˈkəʊdʒənt | inandırıcı, tutarlı, məntiqli
n: cogency = inandırıcılıq, tutarlılıq
adv: cogently = inandırıcı şəkildə
- a cogent rebuttal = tutarlı təkzib :: rebuttal = a statement that says a claim is false
- a cogent thesis = tutarlı tezis :: thesis = a statement or opinion that is discussed logically
- a cogent exposition = aydın və inandırıcı şərh :: exposition = a full explanation of a theory or plan
- a cogent synthesis = bitkin (inandırıcı) sintez :: synthesis = a combination of ideas into a whole
- a cogent appraisal = əsaslandırılmış qiymətləndirmə :: appraisal = a judgement of the value or quality of something
- a cogent commentary = tutarlı şərh :: commentary = a written explanation or discussion of something
- cogent prose = aydın və inandırıcı nəsr :: prose = writing that is not poetry
- cogent and concise = tutarlı və yığcam :: concise = giving only the necessary information in few words
> She presented a cogent case for reform. = O, islahatın zəruriliyini tutarlı şəkildə əsaslandırdı.
> The article offers a cogent rebuttal of the minister's claims. = Məqalədə nazirin iddialarının tutarlı təkzibi verilir.
> A good essay is cogent and concise. = Yaxşı esse tutarlı və yığcam olur.
> He argued his point cogently. = O, fikrini inandırıcı şəkildə əsaslandırdı.
! COGENT describes an argument, reason or piece of writing that is clear, logical and convincing. It is a term of praise in academic contexts: "a cogent argument", "cogent reasons". The "g" is soft: /ˈkəʊdʒənt/. Compare CONVINCING and PERSUASIVE (making you believe), COMPELLING (so strong that you must accept it) and COHERENT (logically connected, though not necessarily convincing).

@ CONFLATE | verb | kənˈfleɪt | qarışdırmaq, (iki fərqli şeyi) eyniləşdirmək
forms: conflate – conflates – conflated – conflated – conflating
n: conflation = qarışdırma, eyniləşdirmə
- conflate with = nə iləsə qarışdırmaq (eyniləşdirmək) :: conflate A WITH B
- conflate the two = bu iki anlayışı qarışdırmaq :: A very common pattern
- conflate religion with ethnicity = dini etnik mənsubiyyətlə eyniləşdirmək :: religion = belief in a god; ethnicity = belonging to a particular people
- conflate journalism with entertainment = jurnalistikanı əyləncə ilə qarışdırmaq :: journalism = the work of reporting news; entertainment = films, shows and other amusements
- conflate equality with uniformity = bərabərliyi eynilik ilə qarışdırmaq :: equality = having the same rights; uniformity = being the same in every way
- conflate separate phenomena = ayrı-ayrı hadisələri qarışdırmaq :: separate = different, not connected; phenomena = facts or events (singular: phenomenon)
- conflate two incidents = iki hadisəni bir-birinə qarışdırmaq :: incident = something that happens, especially something unpleasant
- a lazy conflation = səthi (düşünülməmiş) eyniləşdirmə :: lazy = not making enough effort
> People often conflate wealth with success. = İnsanlar çox vaxt var-dövləti uğurla eyniləşdirirlər.
> Privacy and secrecy are different things; we should not conflate the two. = Şəxsi həyatın toxunulmazlığı ilə gizlilik fərqli şeylərdir; bu ikisini qarışdırmamalıyıq.
> The witness seems to have conflated two incidents. = Deyəsən, şahid iki hadisəni bir-birinə qarışdırıb.
> It is a mistake to conflate equality with uniformity. = Bərabərliyi eynilik ilə qarışdırmaq səhvdir.
! To CONFLATE is to combine two or more separate ideas, texts or sets of information into one, usually wrongly. It is a precise critical verb for essays: "The author conflates correlation with causation." Patterns: conflate A WITH / AND B; conflate the two. Compare CONFUSE (mistake one thing for another), EQUATE (treat as equal) and MERGE (join together, neutral).

@ CONSPICUOUS | adjective | kənˈspɪkjuəs | gözə çarpan, diqqəti çəkən
adj: inconspicuous = gözə çarpmayan, diqqət çəkməyən
adv: conspicuously = gözə çarpacaq dərəcədə
- a conspicuous absence = gözə çarpan yoxluq :: absence = the fact of not being present
- a conspicuous display = nümayişkaranə göstəriş :: display = an arrangement or show of something
- conspicuous bravery = xüsusi (fərqlənən) igidlik :: bravery = the quality of being brave
- a conspicuous exception = gözə çarpan istisna :: exception = a thing that does not follow a rule
- conspicuous extravagance = gözə soxulan israfçılıq :: extravagance = spending more money than is necessary
- a conspicuous omission = gözə çarpan çatışmazlıq (buraxılma) :: omission = something that has not been included
- a conspicuous tattoo = gözə çarpan döymə :: tattoo = a picture marked permanently on the skin
- an inconspicuous doorway = gözə çarpmayan giriş (qapı ağzı) :: doorway = the space where a door opens
> The minister was conspicuous by his absence. = Nazirin yoxluğu dərhal gözə çarpırdı.
> He was awarded a medal for conspicuous bravery. = O, xüsusi igidliyə görə medalla təltif olundu.
> There is one conspicuous omission from the list. = Siyahıda bir gözə çarpan çatışmazlıq var.
> The entrance is an inconspicuous doorway between two shops. = Giriş iki mağaza arasında gözə çarpmayan bir qapıdır.
! CONSPICUOUS means easy to see or notice, often because of being different. "Conspicuous by its / his absence" is a fixed ironic phrase: noticed because it is not there. CONSPICUOUS CONSUMPTION is buying expensive things in order to impress others – a term from sociology. The opposite is INCONSPICUOUS. Compare NOTICEABLE (easy to see), PROMINENT (important or well known) and OBTRUSIVE (noticeable in an unpleasant way).

@ CORROBORATE | verb | kəˈrɒbəreɪt | təsdiq etmək, (sübutla) möhkəmləndirmək
forms: corroborate – corroborates – corroborated – corroborated – corroborating
n: corroboration = təsdiq, əlavə sübut
adj: corroborative = təsdiqedici
- corroborate a version = hadisənin (kiminsə) versiyasını təsdiq etmək :: version = one person's description of an event
- corroborate an alibi = alibini təsdiq etmək :: alibi = evidence that somebody was elsewhere when a crime happened
- corroborate allegations = ittihamları (iddiaları) təsdiq etmək :: allegation = a statement, made without proof, that somebody has done wrong
- corroborate an assertion = iddianı (fikri) təsdiq etmək :: assertion = a statement that you strongly believe is true
- corroborate a recollection = xatirəni (yadda qalanı) təsdiq etmək :: recollection = a memory of something
- corroborate an estimate = təxmini hesablamanı təsdiq etmək :: estimate = a judgement of size or cost made without exact figures
- without corroboration = təsdiqedici sübut olmadan :: Formal fixed phrase

> The court will not accept such a claim without corroboration. = Məhkəmə belə bir iddianı təsdiqedici sübut olmadan qəbul etməyəcək.
! CORROBORATE means to provide evidence that supports a statement, theory or account. It is formal and typical of legal and academic language; it is often passive: "be corroborated BY". Compare CONFIRM (show that something is definitely true), SUBSTANTIATE (provide proof for a claim), VERIFY (check that something is correct) and VALIDATE (show to be acceptable or correct).

@ DEARTH | noun | dɜːθ | qıtlıq, çatışmazlıq
- a dearth of = nəyinsə qıtlığı (çatışmazlığı) :: Always in this pattern: a dearth OF + noun
- a dearth of nurses = tibb bacılarının çatışmazlığı :: nurse = a person trained to care for sick people
- a dearth of imagination = təxəyyül qıtlığı :: imagination = the ability to form new ideas or pictures in your mind
- a dearth of rainfall = yağıntı qıtlığı :: rainfall = the amount of rain that falls
- a dearth of engineers = mühəndis çatışmazlığı :: engineer = a person who designs machines, roads or bridges
- a dearth of amenities = məişət xidmətlərinin (şəraitin) çatışmazlığı :: amenity = a useful feature of a place, such as a shop or park
- a dearth of vacancies = boş iş yerlərinin azlığı :: vacancy = a job that is available
- a chronic dearth = xroniki qıtlıq :: chronic = lasting for a long time; difficult to cure
> There is a dearth of reliable information on the subject. = Bu mövzuda etibarlı məlumat qıtlığı var.
> Rural hospitals suffer from a chronic dearth of nurses. = Kənd xəstəxanaları tibb bacılarının xroniki çatışmazlığından əziyyət çəkir.
> The new suburb has a dearth of amenities. = Yeni şəhərətrafı qəsəbədə məişət xidmətləri çatışmır.
> A dearth of rainfall has ruined the harvest. = Yağıntı qıtlığı məhsulu məhv edib.
! DEARTH is a formal noun used almost only in the pattern "a dearth OF something", meaning that there is not enough of it. It is singular and takes a singular verb: "There is a dearth of evidence." Pronunciation: /dɜːθ/, rhyming with "earth". Compare LACK (general), SHORTAGE (not enough of something needed), SCARCITY (a situation in which something is hard to obtain) and PAUCITY (very formal). The opposite idea is PLETHORA or ABUNDANCE.

@ ELUCIDATE | verb | ɪˈluːsɪdeɪt | aydınlaşdırmaq, izah edib açıqlamaq
forms: elucidate – elucidates – elucidated – elucidated – elucidating
n: elucidation = aydınlaşdırma, izah
- elucidate the mechanism = mexanizmi aydınlaşdırmaq :: mechanism = the way in which something works
- elucidate the pathway = (prosesin) yolunu aydınlaşdırmaq :: pathway = a series of stages or reactions leading to a result
- elucidate an obscure verse = anlaşılmaz misranı izah etmək :: obscure = difficult to understand; verse = a line or group of lines of poetry
- elucidate a riddle = tapmacanı (müəmmanı) açmaq :: riddle = a question that is difficult to understand and has a clever answer
- elucidate the intricacies = incə məqamları aydınlaşdırmaq :: intricacy = one of the complicated details of something
- elucidate the dynamics = dinamikanı (qarşılıqlı təsirləri) aydınlaşdırmaq :: dynamics = the way in which people or things react to each other
- elucidate a metaphor = metaforanı izah etmək :: metaphor = a way of describing something by comparing it to something else
- elucidate the context = konteksti aydınlaşdırmaq :: context = the situation in which something happens
> The lecturer used a diagram to elucidate the mechanism. = Mühazirəçi mexanizmi aydınlaşdırmaq üçün sxemdən istifadə etdi.
> Further research is needed to elucidate the dynamics of the process. = Prosesin dinamikasını aydınlaşdırmaq üçün əlavə tədqiqatlar lazımdır.
> The notes elucidate the more obscure verses of the poem. = Qeydlər poemanın daha anlaşılmaz misralarını izah edir.
> Could you elucidate your last point? = Son fikrinizi aydınlaşdıra bilərsinizmi?
! ELUCIDATE is a formal verb meaning to make something clear by explaining it more fully. It takes a direct object or stands alone: "Let me elucidate." It is typical of academic writing and scientific papers ("elucidate the mechanism"). Compare CLARIFY (make easier to understand), EXPLAIN (general) and ILLUMINATE (make clearer, often by giving new information). The root is the Latin "lucidus" (clear), as in LUCID.
